  above are as first written): the four 10 ha runs of round 12 scored here are from before the round
  13 fixes (docs/handoff/2026-10-20-round13.md, Done). Their inputs were affected by A1, A4 and A5.
  A1: distance_to_water saw no water outside the ROI (in Angul all 134 stands read the 10 km cap,
  and the band was dropped as constant). A4: the Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2 date windows stopped on
  30 Dec 2022, not 31 Dec, which left out one Sentinel-1 scene of 31 Dec 2022 over the Angul,
  Dhenkanal and Kendujhar boxes and two Sentinel-2 scenes over the Koraput boxes (read-only call 4
  of that record). A5: slope and aspect were whole degrees, and a flat cell read as facing north.
  The merge and clustering fixes reach these runs too: B13 (the NDVI phase's cosine was
  log-transformed in every run, its sine too in Angul; since the fix neither is), B14 (a stand in
  several pieces got one mean per piece, not one per stand), B16 (the constant-band test and the
  scaling used histogram quartiles, not exact ones), B17 (the merge counted a superpixel's pixels
  and its valid pixels by different rules at the ROI's edge) and B18 (the merge tested its area
  limits on a sum of floats). Of what this file reads, A4, B17 and B18 can move the stand outlines,
  and so every score; A1, A5, B13, B14 and B16 reach only the stand types, and so only the two
  labelling scores. A6 changes the layers' area_ha and n_pixels, which no score reads. How far any
  of these moves a stand, a type or a score was not measured: it needs Earth Engine. This file is
  kept as the record from before the fixes; these runs are to be re-run (roadmap R3). The box_runs
  row scores the 13 Oct box runs, hand-crafted runs from before the fixes too. Counts per run are in
  docs/reproducibility/round12_runs/README.md. No number in this file is changed.
