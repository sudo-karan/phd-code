B — the growth rate the temporal hypothesis requires
================================================================================================
  zero plots carrying both a date and a field height: 122
  their field Lorey's height: 0.16 .. 33.57 m   median 5.85 m

  A Meta 0 means 'predicted under half a metre'. So for a zero plot measuring H m
  at survey time, the temporal explanation requires (H - 0.5) / gap metres a year,
  sustained from the imagery date to the survey date.

  --- imagery = 2020: late end of the documented 'chiefly 2018-2020' window ---
  gap to survey: 1.8 .. 2.8 years
  required growth rate (m/yr):  median 2.52   p90 4.77   max 15.43
    plots requiring more than 1 m/yr: 107 of 122 (88%)
    plots requiring more than 2 m/yr:  85 of 122 (70%)
    plots requiring more than 3 m/yr:  40 of 122 (33%)

  --- imagery = 2009: earliest acquisition anywhere in the collection ---
  gap to survey: 12.8 .. 13.8 years
  required growth rate (m/yr):  median 0.40   p90 0.88   max 2.52
    plots requiring more than 1 m/yr:   7 of 122 (6%)
    plots requiring more than 2 m/yr:   1 of 122 (1%)
    plots requiring more than 3 m/yr:   0 of 122 (0%)

================================================================================================
What this settles, and what it leaves open
================================================================================================
  The survey ran 2022-04-25 to 2023-04-05, so the field campaign postdates the
  imagery either way and the hypothesis is not symmetric: forest can grow into a
  zero, it cannot grow out of one.

  Under the documented window the hypothesis fails. A ~2-year gap requires the
  median zero plot to have grown at over 2 m a year, and the tallest at over 15,
  which is not growth in any dry deciduous forest.

  Under a 2009 sourcing it survives the arithmetic: a ~13-year gap brings the
  median requirement below half a metre a year. So the verdict rests on these
  particular tiles coming from the late window the collection documents, and the
  residual check is the per-tile acquisition date, not a question for FES.
