  HOW TO READ THIS FILE
  * Chance-level ARI is POSITIVE in this design. Field types and stands both cluster by village, so a stand
    map with no information about the forest still scores above zero. Every statistic is therefore read
    against a spatial ROTATION NULL (odisha_phase2_5_stats.rotation_null): each village's plot cloud moved
    rigidly to a random position and angle inside its village, taking the stand under it. 'pctile' is the
    observed value's mid-rank percentile in that null (share below + half the ties, x100); 'p(>=)' is the
    one-sided (1 + #null >= observed) / (1 + n). Fewer than 100 usable realisations -> NOT ESTIMABLE.
  * Labelling uses a CONDITIONAL null: each village's layer limited to polygons that carry a stand type and
    the labelled plots forced to land on one, because under the unconditional null too few realisations
    label every labelled plot (koraput's merged layer is ~19% untyped).
  * Plot-level permutation and plot-level bootstrap p-values are ANTI-CONSERVATIVE here (plots in a
    village are not independent) and are deliberately NOT reported as tests.
  * The 19 villages (AOI parts) are the units of independence. Arm differences get a paired village
    cluster bootstrap CI and a leave-one-village-out jackknife SE on top of the rotation null.
    (1 of the 20 villages in the AOIs hold only excluded plots, so 19 villages have a plot to score.)
  * Statistics are exactly those of odisha_phase3_1_pairwise_score.py: stands keyed (district, id);
    labelling = plot-weighted mean of per-district ARIs; far-apart = same-district pairs > 2 km apart.
  * MULTIPLE COMPARISONS. 5 statistics x 2 arms = 10 headline tests, plus per-district
    and arm-difference rows. At that count one p near 0.05 is expected from chance alone, so no single
    p-value below is a finding on its own. Holm-adjusted p-values over the headline tests are given at the
    end, together with a plain statement of what the file does and does not show.
  plots in the village AOIs: 267; villages: 20; field types of the 251 plots scored: {1: 108, 2: 76, 3: 15, 4: 52}; far-apart same-district pairs: 9351 before the labelled-plot restriction (each arm's count is in its own table)

WHAT THIS SAYS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * 0 of 10 headline tests beat chance after Holm correction at 0.05.
  * Labelling sits at pctile 34.3 (10 ha), 31.3 (3 ha) of its chance level: indistinguishable from a randomly placed stand map.
  * Delineation on multi-plot stands sits at pctile 72.1 (10 ha), 54.7 (3 ha): inside the null's central 90% in both arms.
  * Arm difference: 5 of 5 statistics differ between 3 ha and 10 ha by no more than
    their chance levels already differ ((i) inside the null's central 95%).
  * So neither ceiling groups or labels structurally alike plots better than chance, and the data cannot
    tell the two ceilings apart. With 19 villages as the units of independence it has little power to.
  * This is NOT the primary analysis (excl0); read it beside the primary results file, not in place of it.
  * NOT supported by this file: 'better than chance' from ARI > 0 (chance is positive here); any single
    p-value read without the Holm column; any plot-level p-value; the bootstrap CI read without (i).
