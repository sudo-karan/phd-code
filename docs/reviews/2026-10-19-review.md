### A1. distance_to_water only sees water inside the ROI
- **Where:** `src/fmu/stages/masking.py:469-473`; `src/fmu/stages/features_static.py:110-121`; `src/fmu/utils/caching.py:302-307` (cached `water_mask` exported over `region=roi`).
- **What is wrong:** The JRC layer is clipped to the ROI before the threshold. `unmask(0)` keeps the clipped footprint, so the 10 km `fastDistanceTransform` never sees water outside the ROI. Every value is therefore min(distance to water inside the ROI, 10 km). The docstring (`features_static.py:9-11`), `docs/current_flow.md:238` and the register's premise in `spec-distance-to-water-left-to-drop-where-it-has-no-spread` ("stands 10 km or more from water") all assume the true distance.
- **Evidence:** In the Angul `_r9` box layer (`docs/reproducibility/r9_runs/odisha_v120_3ha_squares_angul_keptland_s1desc_r9_stands_merged.geojson`), all 219 stands read exactly 10,000 m. The 10 km tile run's config differs from it only in `name`, `description` and `roi`. Its layer (off git, SHA-256 checked against its `_files_off_git.json`) puts the 178 box stands it covers 847–3,567 m from water (median 2,229 m). Angul's clustering manifest has `n_dropped_constant: 1`, the band dropped as constant being `distance_to_water`; the other box runs and the tile have 0. At the cap: Dhenkanal 279/552, Koraput 1,149/1,616, Kendujhar 0/547. Confirmed by three reviewers and four refuters independently, and by me.
- **What changes:** For certain, `distance_to_water` in the Angul `_r9` hand-crafted layer. Very likely Angul's stand types too (the band would become active), and with them the hand-crafted labelling scores of record (`keptland_s1desc_r9_scores.*`, Angul labelling ARI +0.1360) and its chance files. In Dhenkanal and Koraput the capped values are upper bounds, and how many are artefacts is unmeasured. The AlphaEarth arm does not use the band. In November it biases every pixel within about 10 km of a district's edge, and about 89% of the planned 30 km tile.
- **Severity:** A. **Confidence:** high.
- **Suggested fix:** Build the transform's water input over the ROI buffered by `max_water_distance_pixels × analysis_scale_m` (and cache it over that region), clipping only the result. Put this behind a setting that changes the step keys. Then declare and re-run the box runs and compare in a separate commit.

### A2. The declared median/IQR scaling (DEC-003) has no effect: `wekaKMeans` min-max-normalises every band over the fit rows
- **Where:** `src/fmu/stages/clustering.py:1250-1255` (the fit), `:1019-1082` (`_apply_scaling`). Also wrong as a result: `k_elbow.py:3-4` ("the scaled features k-means sees"), `export.py:11-12` ("exactly what k-means saw"), and the silhouette in `metrics.py:385-451`. The register entry is `dn-median-iqr-scaling-dec-003` (`docs/decisions.yaml:1918-1934`).
- **What is wrong:** Weka's Euclidean distance rescales each attribute to [0, 1] by its min and max over the training rows, which cancels any per-band (x − median)/IQR. So k-means weights each band by 1/range², which outliers set: the sensitivity DEC-003 was adopted to avoid. The EE API has no switch for it.
- **Evidence:** The labels are rebuilt from the committed AlphaEarth `_r9` layers, which carry no cyclic bands, so the rows rebuild exactly. A converged k-means should leave about 0 fit rows nearer another centroid in the space it was fitted in. In min-max space over the fit rows: 0–1 rows off per run (Dhenkanal 0/422, Koraput 1/1,331). In the declared IQR space: 4–42 off, and Lloyd's algorithm moves 4–126 rows. Effective band weights (IQR/range) vary 2.3× to 704× within a run (Koraput v120 `distance_to_water` 0.001). Re-typing the AlphaEarth stands in the declared space changes 5–206 stands per district. It moves the primary labelling score from +0.0411 to +0.0426 and far_gap from −0.0043 to −0.0075 (Angul +0.1174 → +0.0978, Dhenkanal +0.0740 → +0.1047). Chance p goes 0.514 → 0.627 and 0.705 → 0.879; Holm p is 1.000 either way.
- **Severity:** A (the committed stand types were not fitted in the declared space; no Holm verdict changes). **Confidence:** high.
- **Suggested fix:** Decide before November. Either amend DEC-003 and the three docstrings to say k-means runs on min-max-normalised post-log bands, and move `k_elbow` and the silhouette into that space. Or keep DEC-003 by fitting outside `wekaKMeans` (client-side on the rows `k_elbow` already reads) and assigning by an explicit distance.

### A3. Left-skewed bands are log-transformed, although DEC-004 declares right-skewed only, and the log makes them more skewed
- **Where:** `src/fmu/stages/clustering.py:945` (`abs(skew_val) > threshold`); `docs/decisions.yaml:1894-1902` (`dn-log-transform-skewed-dec-004`: "Log-transform right-skewed bands"); `docs/design_notes.md:513-519`.
- **What is wrong:** Every band with |skew| > 1 is logged. For a left-skewed band, log(x − min + 0.001) stretches the long low tail instead of compressing it.
- **Evidence:** Over the 11 committed `_r9` runs (skew of per-stand means before and after the recorded offset), 37 of the 88 non-cyclic logged bands were left-skewed, in 10 of the 11 runs. All 37 come out more skewed (median −1.63 → −10.42). Examples: Dhenkanal v120 `vv_p10` −2.84 → −15.70; the 10 km tile `ndvi_mean` −2.42 → −12.70. Changing line 945 to `skew_val > threshold` fails 9 golden tests, because the golden pins the left-skewed `slope` and `A10` as logged. Listed in round 2 (`docs/handoff/2026-10-13-round2.md:443-444`, item B3) and never resolved.
- **Severity:** A (it changes which bands are logged in 10 of 11 committed runs; the size of the effect on types is not measured offline). **Confidence:** high.
- **Suggested fix:** Either test `skew > threshold` as declared, or declare a reflected transform for left-skewed bands. Then re-record the golden, and record each band's skew before and after in `clustering_metadata`.

### A4. The Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2 windows end on 30 Dec 2022, not 31 Dec: `filterDate`'s end is exclusive
- **Where:** `src/fmu/stages/data_load.py:57, 153, 190` (and the window strings recorded at `:125-134`); compare `features_embedding.py:172-179`, which adds one day.
- **What is wrong:** The configs' `end: 2022-12-31` is passed as an exclusive end, so the S2 phenology collection, the S2 composite and the S1 collection stop at 2022-12-30. The register (`dn-date-windows-one-shared-window`, `decisions.yaml:403-405`), `docs/datasets.md:56` and `design_notes.md:97` declare 2017-01-01 to 2022-12-31. The manifests record the declared window, not the one used.
- **Evidence:** The offline request graph sends `DateRange{start '2017-01-01', end '2022-12-31'}`, and the EE reference says the end is exclusive. Phase 1's descending S1 sample (end 2023-01-01, exclusive) counts 189 scenes at every plot inside the Angul, Dhenkanal and Kendujhar boxes and the Angul tile. Those runs' manifests record `s1` = 188. Koraput's counts are not comparable: its run counted 204 scenes over its boxes, while Phase 1 counted 190 at its plots and 205 over the plots' bounds plus 1 km. Koraput is probably on another relative orbit, so no scene is shown to be lost there. Changing the end to `advance(1, 'day')` leaves the `data_load` and `features_radar` step keys unchanged, so a fix would silently reuse the cache.
- **What changes:** The S1 percentile bands of the hand-crafted `_r9` runs in Angul, Dhenkanal and Kendujhar. That includes `vv_minus_vh_median`, a SNIC input band, so the stands and scores built on them change too, by an unknown amount. Whether an S2 scene of 31 Dec is lost cannot be checked offline.
- **Severity:** A. **Confidence:** high.
- **Suggested fix:** Make the end inclusive in `data_load` as `features_embedding` does, and bump `STEP_KEY_VERSION` (or add a setting) so cached composites are not reused. Or, if the shorter window is meant, write 2017-01-01 to 2022-12-30 in the register, docs and manifests.

### A5. Slope and aspect are stored and clustered as whole degrees, and a flat cell is encoded as facing north
- **Where:** `src/fmu/stages/features_static.py:101-102` (`ee.Terrain.products` on NASADEM's integer `elevation`, with no `toFloat`); `src/fmu/stages/clustering.py:684-692` and `src/fmu/stages/profiling.py:190-201` (aspect turned into sin/cos per pixel).
- **What is wrong:** The terrain products of the integer DEM come back rounded to whole degrees, so the three smallest gradients (0.93°, 0.99°, 1.36°) all read 1°. A cell with no gradient gets aspect 0, which the sin/cos decomposition encodes exactly like a north-facing slope, (0, 1). Nothing declares either. The live test expects float values ("Allow small floating-point slack", `tests/test_features_static_live.py:133`).
- **Evidence:**
  - In the committed `_r9` and tile layers, all 689 one-pixel stands have whole-number `slope`, `aspect` and `elevation`. Float bands of the same asset keep their decimals (`ndvi_amplitude_annual`: 0 whole). I re-checked the Angul, Dhenkanal and Kendujhar layers here.
  - The aspect values sit on NASADEM's 1-arc-second central differences, rounded: 47, 227, 152, 332, 65 and 245 occur, while the truncated values 46, 226, 151, 331, 64 and 244 never do.
  - All 9 one-pixel stands with slope 0 have aspect 0.
  - The real `_decompose_cyclic_bands`, run on a flat pixel and a due-north pixel, gives (0, 1) for both.
- **What changes:**
  - In the Angul `_r9` hand-crafted layer, seven stands of 1–9 pixels share the rounded minimum slope of exactly 1.0. All seven sit at log(0.001) = −6.91 after the log (B12), and the recorded slope offset is −0.999. With float terrain, stands 31, 32 and 46 would read about 0.928, stand 1 about 0.99, and the offset about −0.927.
  - Drawing scores cannot change, because segmentation and the merge read no terrain band. Whether any stand type behind the labelling scores changes was not measured; the stands that move most hold no plots.
  - Flat cells barely reach the scored boxes (0 of 345 one-pixel stands) but do occur in the 10 km tile (5 of 235) and Sanjay Van (4 of 109). The flat-is-north half therefore matters mainly for the November district runs.
  - The AlphaEarth arm is not affected.
- **Severity:** A (stand attributes and clustering inputs of the committed hand-crafted runs). **Confidence:** high for the values. The integer DEM as the cause is inferred, not read from Earth Engine.
- **Suggested fix:**
  - Compute the terrain on `dem.toFloat()`, and confirm the type with one read-only `bandTypes()`.
  - Before the unit means, give zero-slope pixels a zero aspect vector, or weight aspect sin/cos by slope.
  - Both changes move the `features_static` step key: November configs would pick them up, while the `_r9` configs would read their cached integer assets back.

### A6. Exported stand areas count some holes as area (up to 2.2×)
- **Where:** `src/fmu/stages/export.py:1272-1293` (`_add_geom_attrs`: `area_m2 = geom.area(maxError=1)`, `n_pixels = area_m2 / scale²`), used by all three layer builders. `reduceToVectors`' own pixel `count` is dropped at `:1317-1331`.
- **What is wrong:** For some polygons with a hole that touches the outer ring at a vertex, Earth Engine's `area()` adds the holes instead of subtracting them. So `area_ha` and `n_pixels` of those stands are too large.
- **Evidence:** `sanjay_van_alphaearth_keptland_r9` stand 83 is 18.27 ha / 1,827 px in the layer. Its planar area with the hole subtracted is 8.23 ha, the merge record says 823 px, and its 5.01 ha hole is covered by stands 77 and 94. In the 8 `_r9` merged layers, 52 of 3,816 polygons are off by more than 1% (15.96 ha). Over every committed layer it is 822 polygons and 73 ha. Ring orientation is standard. Committed lines that change: `odisha_phase2_5_results_districts.txt:46` (486.81 / 4,313.70 ha → 486.63 / 4,311.31 ha) and `:409`, `:771`; `odisha_phase2_3_results_districts.txt:74, 104, 194`; `phase3_3ha/odisha_phase2_3_results_districts.txt:74, 119, 123`; 23 area cells in each joined CSV; `PHASE3_STATUS.md:204` (41 → 37 of 1,954 stands over 3 ha). No scored statistic reads `area_ha`.
- **Severity:** A (descriptive numbers only). **Confidence:** high.
- **Suggested fix:** Keep `reduceToVectors`' `count` as `n_pixels`, and set `area_ha` = count × scale² / 10⁴ on the analysis grid. Test it on a committed affected stand.

### A10. The corrected GEDI row (R² 0.088, slope 0.27) includes 25 m cells that only clip the 50 m disc

## Checked and found sound

- **The kept-land join (30 m rule).** It matches the declaration: covered first, lowest id; else the nearest edge within 30 m inclusive, ties to the lowest id. Planar distances in each box's UTM zone agree with geodesic ones to 0.03 m.
- **The five kept-land scores and the checks S1, S2, covered-only and box runs.** They match their declared definitions, and equal the chance test's array scorers on 300 random tables (except B21's edge case).
- **The chance test's placements use the same join as the observed score.** On 16,020 placed plots per arm, across 60 realisations, the two joins disagree 0 times. On 7,810 random positions they also agree.
- **The chance test's statistics.**
  - Placements equal `rotation_null`'s.
  - The feasible-region sampler matches plain rejection (KS p 0.29–0.98).
  - p = (1 + #null ≥ obs)/(1 + n) and the mid-rank percentile are as declared.
  - Holm is applied over the right family: the five tests of one analysis of one arm, estimable tests only, with check C1 kept outside.
  - Every test had 1,999 usable realisations.
  - The seeds are as declared.
- **Seeds and order.**
  - Every random stream in `src/fmu`, `odisha_script` and `scripts` has an explicit seed (crc32 strings, `random_state`, `default_rng(42)`), and `fmu.evaluation` refuses `None`.
  - `wekaKMeans` gets the config's seed, k and init.
  - No result depends on `hash()` or set order. The declared chance test reproduces under `PYTHONHASHSEED=12345`.
- **Per-step cache keys.** A dynamic audit recorded every config attribute each cached stage reads, under 6 config variants, and every read is covered by that stage's step key. Every context input comes from an upstream step. The 369 recorded per-step keys and 23 fingerprints reproduce, and nothing in cached code changed between the `_r9` runs and 5caae3f. The exceptions are B27's tool and the hazards in B11, C40 and C52: a field added later, the working directory, and the untested key graph.
- **The masking stage.** The majority vote and its tie rule, the WorldCover fallback (it decided 0% of every `_r9` ROI), the IndiaSAT year window, and the mask provenance (its summary numbers aside, C31).
- **S2 and S1 loading and features.** S2 cloud masking, S1 orbit and polarisation filters (already in dB), the CHIRPS window (2,160 pentads), and the harmonic regression (design matrix, amplitude, phase convention). `features_embedding`'s inclusive end.
- **Grids.** The analysis grid and UTM zones: no November district straddles 84° E. No categorical layer is resampled other than nearest neighbour.
- **SNIC on kept land.** Seeds, coverage (0 ha uncovered in all 11 `_r9` runs), and the z-score and distance normaliser over kept land (the grid they are measured on aside, C63). The segmentation goldens pass.
- **The merge's logic.**
  - The pass-2 incremental bookkeeping equals a naive recomputation over 4,800 merges.
  - All 23 committed run records satisfy the merge's invariants.
  - `compare()` is the declared conjunctive gate.
  - The merge reproduces between the clustering and export passes in all 11 `_r9` runs.

- **Every task description starts with `config.name`.** The longest is 96 characters. `ACTIVE_STATES` covers every active state in earthengine-api 1.7.35, `listOperations` pages fully, and an uninterrupted batch submits no task twice.
