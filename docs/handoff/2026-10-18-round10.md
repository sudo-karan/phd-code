- **The cost, per stage.** Batch: the sum of `batchEecuUsageSeconds` over the run's tasks in the

  again costs what it cost. Online: Cloud Monitoring's online series over each pass's span (the
  pass's start in the wrapper log to the next pass's start), all of the project's online use in
  that span; online use cannot be split between the stages of one pass. Each divided by 10 for
  EECU-hours per 1,000 ha, with Angul `_r9`'s own (602.55 ha; its bounding box 9,168 ha) beside
  it, measured the same way. As the Task H note shows, the same step is charged up to about a
