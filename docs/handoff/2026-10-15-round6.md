### What step 1 did

- **The cap.** `features_static.cap_distance_to_water`, default false, and left out of the cache
  fingerprint while false. On, `src/fmu/stages/features_static.py` caps `distance_to_water` with
  `min()` at `max_water_distance_pixels` × `export.analysis_scale_m`, 10 km. The off path:
  - `tests/data/features_static_off_calls.json` holds the stage's calls with the setting off,
    recorded from the stage before the change, for three shipped configs;
  - `tests/test_static_distance_cap.py` checks that the calls are the same, and nothing capped;
  - every fingerprint pin passes.
- **The four configs** set the cap. Their fingerprints move: Angul `9ae5f6a1ac` to `fd08561492`,
  Dhenkanal `ed02415c51` to `98cc68ddf5`, Kendujhar `05257abfb4` to `be7ff1cae3`, Koraput
  `0341971814` to `87bc8dcaf0`. The key is in `ALLOWED`, and in `CHANGED` for the box configs.
- **The reuse tool.** The cap is read by `features_static`, so it cannot be one of
  `REUSE_SAFE_KEYS`, which no stage before segmentation reads. It is the one key of a new
  `RECOMPUTE_KEYS`, which names the stage that reads it. Where a config differs from its source
  in such a key, that stage's assets are computed by the run, never copied. The test checks that
  the key is read by `features_static` and by no other module before segmentation. Dry run, offline:
