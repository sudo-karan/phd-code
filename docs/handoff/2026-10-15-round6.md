### Step 5: two read-only reports (nothing fixed)

**a) The AlphaEarth Sanjay Van kept-land stands have every named measurement null.**

- **Why.** The export stage builds the stand layer with the means of one image over each stand
  polygon, `_feature_source_image` in `src/fmu/stages/export.py`: for an AlphaEarth run, the
  embedding alone ("Embedding arm: the single embedding image"); for a hand-crafted run, the
  optical, radar, structure and static images together. So
  `sanjay_van_alphaearth_keptland_stands_merged.geojson` holds A00 to A63 per stand and no
  named measurement. `scripts/build_ui_early_data.py` then writes the seven named means it
  keeps first (`DECIMALS`: `canopy_height`, `canopy_height_std`, `ndvi_amplitude_annual`,
  `vv_minus_vh_median`, `ndvi_mean`, `elevation`, `slope`) for every run, from a stand file
  that does not have them, so they come out null. It is the same in every AlphaEarth run in
  `ui_data/`: `sanjay_van_alphaearth_repro` and the four `odisha_alphaearth_*_repro`.
- **What the run does have.** The AlphaEarth arm computes the optical and structure features
  as well, because the merge's criteria are `canopy_height` and `ndvi_amplitude_annual`; it
  computes no radar or static features. Read-only, in Earth Engine now:
  - `sanjay_van_alphaearth_keptland` (`bc34e9b8d5`): `features_optical/optical_features` (6
    bands), `features_structure/structure_features` (3), `features_embedding/embedding_features`
    (64), and its stand raster `segmentation/snic_clusters`;
  - `sanjay_van_baseline_keptland` (`883920a4d9`), the same rectangle: `features_radar` (9
    bands) and `features_static` (5), with its own optical and structure.

  The feature images are on the same geographic grid (EPSG:4326, about 10 m); the export stage
  already averages them at `scale` 10 m for the baseline. The run record
  (`sanjay_van_alphaearth_keptland_run.json`, `stand_attributes`) also holds two of the seven
  per stand, `canopy_height` and `ndvi_amplitude_annual`: the merge's own means, on the
  analysis grid.
- **What it would take**, three ways:
  1. **Read-only, one reduction:** a script on the pattern of
     `odisha_script/odisha_read_back_export.py` (no task, `ee_read_only.forbid_writes()`):
     `ee.Image.cat` of the AlphaEarth run's optical and structure images and the baseline
     run's radar and static images, `reduceRegions(mean, scale=10)` over the 559 committed

     Locked stage and every later AlphaEarth stand file, and radar and static would still be

**b) In the 13 Oct Koraput box run, `vv_iqr` and `vh_iqr` are 0 on every stand.**

- **Why.** The radar features are statistics, per pixel, over the Sentinel-1 images of the
  radar window (`src/fmu/stages/data_load.py`, `_load_s1`): `COPERNICUS/S1_GRD`, 2017-01-01 to

  default, and what all 39 Odisha configs set). Over the Koraput boxes that leaves **one image**:
  the run's log says "S1 radar window: 1 images after orbit/pol filter", and Earth Engine,
  read-only, lists it as 23 Dec 2018. With one value per pixel, the 10th, 50th and 90th
  percentiles are the same, and `vv_iqr` and `vh_iqr`, which `features_radar.py` computes as
  p90 minus p10 (an inter-decile range, under the name IQR), are 0 on every pixel. In the stand
  file of 13 Oct, all 2,401 polygons have `vv_iqr` = `vh_iqr` = 0 and p10 = p50 = p90 in both
  polarisations; the clustering drops the two bands as constant ("dropped 2 constant band(s)
  (zero spread): vh_iqr, vv_iqr"). The kept-land run reuses the same radar asset, so the same.
  The other radar bands there are that one date's backscatter, not a six-year statistic.
- **The descending orbit** has 204 images over the Koraput boxes in the same window.
- **The other districts**, ascending against descending: Kendujhar 2 against 188 (5 May 2019,
  30 May 2021), Angul 3 against 188, Dhenkanal 174 against 188; Sanjay Van, 178 ascending. The

  docstring and `docs/datasets.md` count on "100+ S1 images".
- **What would change it** (not done): `data_load.s1_orbit: DESCENDING` gives 188 to 204 images

  everything after them would be computed again. The docs give the reason for one direction

  entry on it.
