## 5. Questions for Jaskaran

1. **The per-type areas of the profiling stage and the export manifest are counted on a
   geographic grid, so they are too large away from the equator** (older code, Locked stages,
   every run; found this round, not fixed). Both count a type's pixels with `scale` and no
   projection, so on the clustering image's own grid, a geographic one, and take each pixel as
   0.01 ha. At Sanjay Van's latitude those pixels are about 8.8 m wide.
   - In both kept-land runs the six types come to 967.3 ha. They cover 847.5 ha of kept land,
     and the stands' own areas, on the analysis grid, sum to 846.9 ha. 847.5 / cos(28.5°) =
     964.6, so the difference is the grid.
   - The stands, the merge and the clustering are not affected: they count on the analysis grid.
   - Options:
     - (a) Count on the analysis grid in both stages. The numbers then agree with the stands. It
       changes those two stages' numbers in every run, not their maps.
     - (b) Leave it, with the note it now has in `ui_data/by_run/<run>/profiles.json`.
   - Recommendation: (a), with the Odisha round, beside `distance_to_water` (round 4's question
     2): the same kind of fix to Locked stages.
2. **"Over 10 km" for the other runs in `ui_data/`?** The brief asks for it for the two kept-land
   runs. The 18 others still hold the fill value as a number, and the page reads it as "over
   10 km". (a) Leave them. (b) Mark them the same way: the Sanjay Van `_repro` runs now; the
   Odisha ones, which this round does not touch, with the Odisha round. Recommendation: (b).
