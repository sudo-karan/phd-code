### Fix 1: the comment on Xiong's axes, not changed

The brief says Xiong et al. (2024) "merge on mean height and dominant-species proportion; canopy
closure is computed per segment but is not a merge rule", and asks for the comment calling canopy
closure Xiong's "third axis" to be corrected. The paper's merging rule does include canopy
closure, so `src/fmu/config.py` is not changed, and question 2 asks how to go on. Read: the version
of record (Silva Fennica 58(2), article 23014; the publisher's PDF, 18 pages), its web page with
the images of the equations, and the authors' code, which the paper names (read as text, not run).
Crossref (`https://api.crossref.org/works/10.14214/sf.23014`) gives the title "Forest stand
delineation using airborne LiDAR and hyperspectral data" and Hao Xiong as the first person author,
and lists no correction.

- **The merging rule, Eq. 1** (section 2.6.1, PDF page 7), is five conditions: Area_m + Area_n <
  Area_max; TreeSpecies_m = TreeSpecies_n; |CanopyClosure_m - CanopyClosure_n| < 0.2;
  |TreeProportion_m - TreeProportion_n| < TP1; |StandHeight_m - StandHeight_n| < SH1. The sentence
  under it merges a segment with the neighbour "which meets all the above conditions". Canopy
  closure is one of them, with a fixed 0.2.
- **The eliminating rule, Eq. 2** (section 2.6.2, page 8), has no canopy closure term: the area
  under Area_min, the same species, TP2, SH2, and the longest common side.
- **Where the paper leaves closure out:** the recap that closes section 2.6.1 names only the area,
  TP1 and SH1, and it leaves out the same-species condition too, so it is not a full list; section
  2.6.3 sets only the height and species-proportion thresholds (SH1 3, TP1 0.2, SH2 5, TP2 0.5, in
  section 4.1), closure's 0.2 being fixed in the equation; and section 4.2 says the method takes
  into account "such as tree height, tree species composition, forest stands area, and length of
  common edges" (page 13).
- **Where it puts closure in:** section 2.5 computes it per segment (the share of cells with a
  canopy height over 2 m) among the attributes the two rules use; section 3.1 says "The stands with
  similar tree height, density, dominant tree species and proportion were well merged after the
  homogeneous merging step" (page 9); the Highlights say "The forest height, canopy closure, and
  species information were taken into account during the delineation process"; section 4.1 and the
  Conclusions say the same.
- **The authors' code** (`github.com/HaoXplorer/Forest-Stand-Delineation`, last commit `6ffe926`,
  7 Mar 2024) tests closure where it applies Eq. 1's logic: the pair's area under 50 ha or 20 ha,
  the same species, the species-proportion difference, closure within 0.2 (0.1 in one of its two
  branches) and height within 3. That procedure is in the file named
  `eliminating-small-segments.pro`. The file named `merging-homogeneous-segments.pro` holds Eq. 2's
  logic (a segment under 5,000 m², the same species, the proportion within 0.5, height within 5,
  the longest common side) and reads closure without testing it. Read by its file names, the code
  gives the brief's reading.
- **The register reads Eq. 1 the same way**, from the one verifier who could read the equation
  images (the other could not and left the point unsettled) and a later review that read them
  again; the same note is in five entries, among them `spec-merge-default-criteria` and
  `spec-merge-two-pass-hard-gate`.

So the comment's core claim, that canopy closure is one of Xiong's merge conditions and is not
covered here, is what Eq. 1 says; its count of three "axes" is looser than Eq. 1's five conditions
(question 2). The brief's wording would put into a Locked module a statement that the paper's
equation, and the sentence under it, contradict, and CLAUDE.md says never to invent a finding.
