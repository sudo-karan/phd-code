## 3. Blocked

1. **A6, line 3: 3 of the 2,755 superpixels SNIC drew on Kendujhar's kept land are each in
   more than one piece.** Counted as connected pieces of one label (8-connected, as SNIC runs),
   the 2,755 superpixels make 2,758 pieces. The masked path counts on each superpixel being one
   connected piece of kept land: SNIC grows a superpixel only through pixels it can see, and
   with the mask it sees kept land only. Lines 1 and 2 hold, so no superpixel is joined across
   removed land by pixels it labelled there.

   Why it matters. Superpixels are what the merge joins, and the merge treats each one as a
   single region. A superpixel in two pieces gives a stand in two pieces, and through it two
   kept patches that do not touch can end up in one stand, which the masked design rules out.

   What is not known, because I stopped (none of this was run):
   - where the three superpixels are, how big each piece is, and how far apart the pieces of one
     superpixel lie;
   - whether SNIC does the same without the mask. The check counts pieces on the masked run only;
     the box run's own cached superpixels (whole area,
     `projects/replicating-paper/assets/fmu/odisha_v120_3ha_squares_kendujhar/segmentation/snic_clusters__2a2e6e38e3`)
     would answer it with the same count, read-only;
   - the cause. Two candidates, neither checked: Earth Engine computes SNIC tile by tile, with a
     margin of `neighborhoodSize` (128 px here) around each tile, so a superpixel near a tile
     edge could be grown differently in two tiles and come out as one label in two places; or the
     count itself (`measure_masked` in `scripts/check_snic_on_kept_land.py` runs
     `connectedComponents` with maxSize 256 on the stage's label image).

   What could unblock it. The brief says Claude (chat) changes the code before anything runs in
   batch; Claude (chat) and Jaskaran decide which:

     superpixels of the same run;

## 6. Notes for whoever does step 1 (none of this is in the code yet)

Read while planning step 1, from the code at `12d2a05`. They are notes, not decisions.

- **D2, the soft ceiling.** Pass 2 is `merge_superpixels` in `src/fmu/utils/region_merge.py`:
  the ceiling binds where it builds `fitting` (neighbours with `area(r) + area(nb) <=
  max_area_ha`) and again where it files a leftover stand as `area_blocked`; both need the
  setting. Two readings to settle:
  - *The bound.* "The result may exceed max_area_ha" has no upper limit as written. A region
    below the minimum joining a neighbour of at most `max_area_ha` gives less than
    `max_area_ha + min_area_ha`, which is the cap D2 sets. But a neighbour already over the
    ceiling (after an earlier soft-ceiling merge) could take a second small region and go past
    that cap, and `reduceConnectedComponents` would then delete it. Proposal: bound every
    soft-ceiling merge at `max_area_ha + min_area_ha`, the cap itself, so the test D2 asks for
    holds by construction.
  - *Which neighbour.* (A) the best neighbour among all of them, the ceiling not entering the
    choice (my reading of "may join its best neighbour even if the result exceeds"); or (B) the
    ceiling first, and the soft ceiling only when no neighbour fits under it. (B) makes fewer
    stands over the ceiling. Worth one line from Jaskaran.
  - "Only regions below the minimum": the region's area at its turn, not when the sweep listed
    it (it can grow during the sweep when `pass2_skip_at_min_area` is false, as in the Sanjay Van
    configs).
  - Other places that assume the hard ceiling: `Config.max_component_pixels()` and its
    docstring, the module docstring of `region_merge.py` ("the fallback still respects
    `max_area_ha`"), the `area_blocked` warning in `src/fmu/stages/merge.py`, and the fake merge
    parameters in `tests/test_mask_before_snic.py` and `tests/test_merge_stage.py`. The 200
    seeded graphs of `tests/test_region_merge_provenance.py` pin the default path's stands.
- **D3, specks out of the fit.** `_train_and_apply_kmeans` in `src/fmu/stages/clustering.py`
  samples one row per unit three times: for the skewness and log offsets, for the scaling, and
  for k-means. D3 names the k-means rows. If the specks also left the first sample, a speck below
  the sample minimum would get `log(x - min + 0.001)` of a negative number, a masked pixel, and
  no type, against "they then get the type of the nearest fitted cluster". So: k-means rows only,
  and say so. The merge already marks the stands concerned (`below_min` in `stand_provenance`,
  area in `stand_attributes`, both in the context), so the stage can leave them out by id. The
  coverage count `n_units_dropped_null_band` is computed as habitat units minus training rows;
  it must use the rows before the specks are taken out, or the specks are counted as missing
  band data. The setting needs `merge.enabled`. Keep `clustering_metadata` key for key as it is
  while the setting is off (the summary numbers read it from cached assets).
- **Golden tests.** The way of `tests/test_segmentation_off_golden.py` works for the clustering
  stage too (record its calls from `git show 12d2a05:src/fmu/stages/clustering.py`). For the
