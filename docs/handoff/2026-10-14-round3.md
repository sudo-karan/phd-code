## 6. Notes for whoever does step 1 (none of this is in the code yet)

Read while planning step 1, from the code at `12d2a05`. They are notes, not decisions.

- **D2, the soft ceiling.** Pass 2 is `merge_superpixels` in `src/fmu/utils/region_merge.py`:
  the ceiling binds where it builds `fitting` (neighbours with `area(r) + area(nb) <=
  max_area_ha`) and again where it files a leftover stand as `area_blocked`; both need the
  setting. Two readings to settle:
  - *The bound.* "The result may exceed max_area_ha" has no upper limit as written. A region
    below the minimum joining a neighbour of at most `max_area_ha` gives less than
    `max_area_ha + min_area_ha`, which is the cap D2 sets. But a neighbour already over the
    ceiling (after an earlier soft-ceiling merge) could take a second small region and go past
    that cap, and `reduceConnectedComponents` would then delete it. Proposal: bound every
    soft-ceiling merge at `max_area_ha + min_area_ha`, the cap itself, so the test D2 asks for
    holds by construction.
  - *Which neighbour.* (A) the best neighbour among all of them, the ceiling not entering the
    choice (my reading of "may join its best neighbour even if the result exceeds"); or (B) the
    ceiling first, and the soft ceiling only when no neighbour fits under it. (B) makes fewer
    stands over the ceiling. Worth one line from Jaskaran.
  - "Only regions below the minimum": the region's area at its turn, not when the sweep listed
    it (it can grow during the sweep when `pass2_skip_at_min_area` is false, as in the Sanjay Van
    configs).
  - Other places that assume the hard ceiling: `Config.max_component_pixels()` and its
    docstring, the module docstring of `region_merge.py` ("the fallback still respects
    `max_area_ha`"), the `area_blocked` warning in `src/fmu/stages/merge.py`, and the fake merge
    parameters in `tests/test_mask_before_snic.py` and `tests/test_merge_stage.py`. The 200
    seeded graphs of `tests/test_region_merge_provenance.py` pin the default path's stands.
- **D3, specks out of the fit.** `_train_and_apply_kmeans` in `src/fmu/stages/clustering.py`
  samples one row per unit three times: for the skewness and log offsets, for the scaling, and
  for k-means. D3 names the k-means rows. If the specks also left the first sample, a speck below

  band data. The setting needs `merge.enabled`. Keep `clustering_metadata` key for key as it is
  while the setting is off (the summary numbers read it from cached assets).
