### Task 2 (roadmap 3.2, #44 item 12): stand types from the fit's own row

#### Written first: what a stand with no row in the fit gets, today and after the change

Written before any code was changed.

**How a stand is typed today.** The export stage vectorises the stand labels, one polygon per
connected piece of a stand, and gives each polygon the commonest stand type among the pixels of
the type image (`cluster_labels`) it touches, each pixel weighted by its share inside the polygon
(`reduceRegions` with `ee.Reducer.mode()`, `src/fmu/stages/export.py`,
`_build_merged_feature_collection`, step 3). A polygon that touches no typed pixel gets a null
type.

**Where the types come from.** The clustering stage trains k-means on one row per stand: the
stand's means, sampled at one of its pixels inside the habitat mask where no band is missing. It
then applies the trained clusters to every pixel of the habitat-masked image of per-stand means.
A stand's means are the same at each of its pixels, so every typed pixel of a stand carries the
type the fitted clusters give that stand's row. A stand with a row has typed pixels, all of its
row's type; a stand without a row has none.

**Which stands have no row in the fit, and what each gets.** The counts are from the committed
records of the eleven `_r9` runs (`docs/reproducibility/r9_runs/`, the clustering manifests'
summary numbers) and from the demo set's re-merge (`docs/demo/remerge/*.json`, 13 Oct runs,
legacy cache layout).

1. **No row at all.** The stand has no pixel inside the habitat mask, or a feature band has no
   data over it. In the `_r9` runs, which segment kept land only, no stand lies outside the mask,
   and 2 to 10 stands per run lack a band (`units_dropped_missing_band`). By count, these are the
   stands the layer has no polygon for: merge stands minus that number equals the stands in the
   layer in all eleven runs (Angul hand-crafted: 221 stands, 2 without a row, 219 in the layer).
   - *Today:* the vote over the typed pixels its polygon touches. On the analysis grid, where
     every run since round 9 is cached (the `_r9` runs among them), the polygons' edges lie on the
     type image's pixel edges, so a polygon touches no typed pixel of a neighbour, and a stand with
     no typed pixel of its own gets a null type. On the legacy layout, used before round 9, the
     types lay on a geographic grid and the polygons on UTM, so a polygon could take in slivers of
     its neighbours' typed pixels: a stand with no row then took a neighbour's type. These are the
     "3 to 33 per run" of the brief: 3 to 33 stands per run in the 13 Oct runs, re-merged
     (`stands_typed_by_the_export_only`; 3 to 32 under the old merge rule), as
     `docs/demo/remerge/README.md`, section 4, describes.
   - *After the change as worded* ("a stand with a row in the fit takes that row's type"): the
     rule says nothing about them, so they keep today's vote: null on the analysis grid, a
     neighbour's type or null on the legacy layout. So the change does not touch the 3 to 33. The
     other answer, that a stand with no row is always null, can reasonably be chosen too: it is
     what `docs/outputs.md` ("Null `cluster_id` is meaningful") and the export's own comment ("Null
     where clustering never reached the stand") describe as intended, while
     `odisha_script/PHASE3_ALPHAEARTH_PREDECLARED.md` scored such stands with the type the export
     wrote. **Question 1; left as it is.**
2. **A row, left out of the fit.** With `clustering.fit_excludes_stands_below_min_area` on, as in
   every kept-land run, the stands under 1 ha are left out of the rows k-means is trained on, but
   their rows are typed by the fitted clusters (the declaration's D3: "They get the type of the
   nearest fitted cluster"): 52 to 389 stands per `_r9` run.
   - *Today:* the vote, which on the analysis grid gives them their row's type.
   - *After the change:* whether they have "a row in the fit" can reasonably be read either way:
     they have a row, typed by the fit, but the fit was not trained on it. **Question 2; left as
     it is:** they keep the vote. On the analysis grid both readings give the same type.
3. **No polygon.** Not in the layer, before or after.

**Stands with a row in the fit**, the ones the change is for, take their row's type. Where a
polygon's vote can differ from its row's type, on the legacy layout, it did so for 0 to 3 stands
per run in the demo set's re-merge under the old merge rule and 0 to 4 under the new
(`stands_the_export_types_differently_from_the_fit`). On the analysis grid the vote covers a
stand's own pixels only, so the change should alter no stand there; the count per `_r9` run
