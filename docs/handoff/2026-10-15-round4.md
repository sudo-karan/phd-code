### What step 1 did

- **Q1, the fix** (`src/fmu/utils/kept_land.py`, `src/fmu/stages/segmentation.py`). With the
  setting on, kept land is `habitat_mask` put on the analysis grid and clipped to the ROI with the
  same `reproject(grid).clip(roi)` the stage gives its two outputs (`kept_land_in_roi`), then
  written out as 0 outside the clip. SNIC's input, its seeds, the masks of the z-score and of the
  distance normaliser, the no-kept-land guard, the outputs' mask and the merge's kept-land
  coverage are all made from it. With the setting on the stage makes its one analysis grid first;
  off, it makes it after SNIC, as before. No new setting, no fingerprint moved, and the off path's
  Earth Engine calls are those recorded at `983dbe7` (`tests/test_segmentation_off_golden.py`).
  `scripts/check_snic_on_kept_land.py` and `scripts/check_split_superpixels.py` build kept land
  the same way, so that step 3 checks what SNIC is given. Register entries
  `spec-segmentation-mask-before-snic` and `spec-keptland-configs-mask-before-snic`.
- **Why the 0 outside the clip.** After the clip, kept land is masked outside the ROI, and SNIC's
  input is then masked there only if `updateMask` treats a masked pixel of its mask image as
  invalid, which the Earth Engine reference does not say. A 0 in a mask masks by a documented
  rule. `unmask(0, False)` fills only pixels whose mask is 0, so no pixel inside the clip changes,
  the clip's partial masks along the edge included. The first reviewer proposed it and then
  checked it (below). It keeps Jaskaran's "same clip": the clip is the outputs' clip, and the
  edge pixels are treated as the outputs treat them.
